# Concepts

The model moves from an observed **execution**, through **provenance** and
approved **evidence**, to a **decision**, qualified **attribution**, and a
policy-filtered **explanation**.

## Kid-level view

Think of a school project: write down what happened, where each fact came
from, which choice was made, why it was allowed to be shown, and what is still
uncertain.

## Production view

Canonical artifacts are typed, reference-oriented records with schema identity,
timestamps or sequence information, policy outcomes, and method limitations.
They are inputs to renderers and adapters, not a substitute for authorization
or an application audit log.
